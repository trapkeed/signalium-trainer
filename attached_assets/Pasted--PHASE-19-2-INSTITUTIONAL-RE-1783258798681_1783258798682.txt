======================================================================
PHASE 19.2
INSTITUTIONAL RESEARCH SCORING ENGINE (IRSE)
======================================================================

Extend the Institutional Research Intelligence Platform.

MISSION

Build Signalium's Institutional Research Scoring Engine (IRSE).

The objective is to create a standardized quantitative scoring framework
used to evaluate every experiment, model, dataset, feature set,
validation run, and production deployment.

The Research Score becomes the official ranking metric across the
entire Signalium ecosystem.

Every experiment must receive a reproducible composite score.

The scoring system must be configurable and transparent.

Maintain complete backward compatibility.

======================================================================
RESEARCH SCORE
======================================================================

Implement a composite Research Score ranging from 0 to 100.

The score must be calculated from configurable weighted categories.

Example default weighting:

Validation Quality ............... 25%

Trading Performance .............. 25%

Robustness ....................... 15%

Calibration ...................... 10%

Generalization ................... 10%

Efficiency ....................... 5%

Explainability ................... 5%

Operational Readiness ............ 5%

Allow administrators to change weights without modifying code.
