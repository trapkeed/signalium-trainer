======================================================================
PHASE 19.3
HIERARCHICAL RESEARCH SCORING ARCHITECTURE (HRSA)
======================================================================

Repository:

signalium-trainer

======================================================================
MISSION
======================================================================

Upgrade the Institutional Research Scoring Engine.

Replace the flat weighted scoring model with a hierarchical institutional
research scoring architecture.

The objective is to make the scoring engine transparent, extensible,
auditable, deterministic and suitable for long-term quantitative
research.

Maintain complete backward compatibility.

======================================================================
SCORING ARCHITECTURE
======================================================================

Implement three scoring layers.

LEVEL 1

Raw Metrics

↓

LEVEL 2

Category Scores

↓

LEVEL 3

Institutional Research Score

No overall score may be calculated directly from raw metrics.

All scores must flow through the hierarchy.
