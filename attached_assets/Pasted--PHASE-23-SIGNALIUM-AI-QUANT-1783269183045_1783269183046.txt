======================================================================
MISSION
======================================================================

Build Signalium's AI Quant Research Analyst.

This is an institutional research assistant powered by a Large Language
Model (LLM).

Its responsibility is to help researchers understand the behavior of
Signalium's quantitative platform.

It must NEVER generate BUY or SELL signals.

It must NEVER override production forecasting.

It must NEVER replace deterministic model logic.

It is a research advisor only.

Maintain complete backward compatibility.
