Signalium Trainer Phase S1
Repository: signalium-trainer

Objective:

Preserve critical capital protection infrastructure while migrating to the One Brain architecture.

The following systems must remain operational:

1. Drawdown Protection
2. Broker Validation
3. Exposure Protection
4. Shadow Observation
5. Deployment Rollback

These systems are NOT decision makers.

They exist purely as safety infrastructure.

--------------------------------------------------
DRAWNDOWN MANAGER
--------------------------------------------------

Keep:

trainer/icari/drawdown_manager.py

Convert its role from:

decision override engine

to:

capital circuit breaker.

Allowed actions:

- reduce global risk multiplier
- pause new trade creation
- emergency account lockdown
- disable scaling
- disable pyramiding

Forbidden actions:

- changing direction
- modifying stop loss
- modifying take profit
- changing targets
- changing confidence
- changing hold duration

The QDE remains sole decision maker.

--------------------------------------------------
BROKER SAFETY
--------------------------------------------------

Broker adapters may reject:

- invalid lot sizes
- insufficient margin
- invalid stop distances
- unsupported symbols
- market closed states

Broker adapters must never modify:

- entries
- exits
- targets
- confidence
- risk signal

--------------------------------------------------
PORTFOLIO SAFETY
--------------------------------------------------

Keep exposure protection only as:

- portfolio VaR monitor
- correlated exposure monitor
- concentration monitor

Allowed actions:

- pause new trades
- reduce account risk multiplier

Forbidden actions:

- modifying model decisions
- changing trade direction
- changing targets

--------------------------------------------------
SHADOW MODE
--------------------------------------------------

Keep:

Shadow inference.

Remove:

- expert voting
- ensemble routing
- canary deployment
- expert weighting

Shadow inference behavior:

Live market snapshot
    -> Champion predicts
    -> Trade executes

Same snapshot
    -> Challenger predicts
    -> Prediction stored

Research Lab compares:

- direction accuracy
- confidence calibration
- MFE
- MAE
- drawdown
- management quality
- latency

No challenger may place trades.

--------------------------------------------------
ROLLBACK

Keep automatic rollback capability.

Rollback restores:

- active model
- artifact
- metadata
- feature contract
- output schema

Generate migration report.

Generate architecture report.
