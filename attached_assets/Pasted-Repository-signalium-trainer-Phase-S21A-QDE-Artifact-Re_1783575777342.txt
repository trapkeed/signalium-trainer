Repository:
signalium-trainer

Phase:
S21A — QDE Artifact Registry, Deployment Infrastructure and Version Distribution

Objective:

Transform signalium-trainer into the official producer and publisher of deployable QDE versions.

The trainer owns:

* training
* validation
* certification
* promotion
* deployment approval
* artifact generation
* rollback approval

The trainer never executes trades.

---

1. ARTIFACT MANIFEST SYSTEM

---

Implement deployment manifests for every trained QDE version.

Generate:

qde_manifest.json

Fields:

model_version
artifact_hash
dataset_version
feature_version
training_commit
calibration_version
training_timestamp
certification_status
promotion_status
supported_symbols
supported_tasks
export_formats
framework_version

The manifest becomes the authoritative source of truth for deployments.

---

2. MODEL EXPORT PIPELINE

---

Automatically export:

TorchScript artifacts
ONNX artifacts
configuration files
calibration files
metadata files
deployment manifests

Generate SHA256 checksums for all exports.

---

3. ARTIFACT REGISTRY

---

Create persistent artifact storage.

Store:

weights
configs
calibration artifacts
validation reports
certification reports
research reports
manifests
metadata

All artifacts must be immutable and versioned.

---

4. DEPLOYMENT API

---

Expose deployment endpoints:

GET /deployment/active
GET /deployment/version/{id}
GET /deployment/manifest/{id}
GET /deployment/download/{id}
GET /deployment/history

POST /deployment/promote
POST /deployment/rollback

Promotion endpoints must require admin authorization.

---

5. PROMOTION WORKFLOW

---

Supported states:

DRAFT
TRAINING
VALIDATING
SHADOW
CANDIDATE
ACTIVE
RETIRED

Only one ACTIVE QDE may exist.

---

6. ROLLBACK SYSTEM

---

Implement instant rollback support.

Rollback must:

deactivate current model
activate previous model
restore deployment metadata

Rollback target time:

under 10 seconds.

---

7. SHADOW OBSERVATION

---

Allow candidate models to receive:

market snapshots
trade telemetry
decision telemetry
position telemetry

Shadow models never influence live trading.

Observation only.

---

8. ARTIFACT SIGNING

---

Generate:

artifact signatures
manifest signatures
integrity hashes

Validate signatures before promotion.

---

9. DEPLOYMENT AUDIT TRAIL

---

Persist:

promotion history
rollback history
deployment history
artifact history
approval history

Nothing is deleted.

---

10. ADMIN DEPLOYMENT CENTER

---

Research Lab administrators can:

view artifacts
approve promotions
approve rollbacks
inspect manifests
inspect validation reports

No public access.

---

11. ARCHITECTURAL RULES

---

signalium-trainer produces QDE versions.

signalium-trainer never executes trades.

signalium-trainer never communicates with brokers.

signalium-trainer never manages positions.

signalium-trainer publishes deployable intelligence only.

Implement production-grade code only.

Avoid placeholders.

Maintain reproducibility and auditability.
