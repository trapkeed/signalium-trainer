Repository:

signalium-trainer

======================================================================
MISSION
======================================================================

Build Signalium's Institutional AutoML and Research Optimization Platform.

The objective is to automatically discover better-performing model configurations while integrating tightly with the existing trainer, model registry, experiment tracking, and Institutional Research Validation Platform.

Do NOT modify production models directly.

All candidate models must be trained and validated before promotion.

Maintain complete backward compatibility.
