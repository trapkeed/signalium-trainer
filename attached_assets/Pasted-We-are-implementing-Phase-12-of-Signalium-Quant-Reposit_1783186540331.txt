We are implementing Phase 12 of Signalium Quant.

Repository:

signalium

The Signalium Trainer (signalium-trainer) already exists and is responsible for:

• Downloading datasets
• Training models
• Evaluating models
• Exporting TorchScript/ONNX artifacts
• Uploading trained models

The Signalium platform is responsible for:

• Receiving trained models
• Validating them
• Registering them
• Versioning them
• Deploying them
• Managing their lifecycle

Do NOT modify the trainer repository.

This phase belongs entirely inside the Signalium platform.

======================================================================
MISSION
======================================================================

Build a production-grade AI Model Registry.

The Model Registry becomes the authoritative source for every AI model used by Signalium.

Every uploaded model must be validated, versioned, audited and tracked throughout its lifecycle.

The registry must support future deployment, rollback, shadow testing and continuous retraining.
