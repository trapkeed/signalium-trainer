======================================================================
MISSION
======================================================================

Build Signalium's Institutional Market Microstructure Intelligence
Engine.

The objective is to understand HOW price moves, not merely WHERE price
may move.

The engine must support multiple data granularities.

Priority order:

1. Existing OHLC data
2. Tick-derived features (future)
3. Level II / DOM data (future)
4. Broker execution statistics (future)

The architecture must degrade gracefully when higher-resolution data is
unavailable.

Maintain full backward compatibility.
