Signalium 2.0

PHASE 27.7

Repository:
signalium-trainer

=========================================================
OBJECTIVE
=========================================================

Integrate the Research Lab as an internal trainer subsystem.

The Research Lab is an administrator-only capability.

No public users may access it.

The Research Lab exists to continuously improve the QDE.

=========================================================
RESEARCH LAB RESPONSIBILITIES
=========================================================

Dataset Creation

Feature Discovery

Experiment Tracking

Model Benchmarking

Backtesting

A/B Evaluation

Parameter Search

Retraining Validation

Deployment Approval

Failure Analysis

Market Regime Analysis

=========================================================
NO FRONTEND
=========================================================

The trainer repository must remain headless.

No frontend application.

No dashboards.

No user interface.

Research functionality must be exposed via internal APIs only.

=========================================================
ADMIN ACCESS
=========================================================

Only administrators may invoke research endpoints.

Signalium admin frontend may consume these APIs.

Retail users may never access research functionality.
