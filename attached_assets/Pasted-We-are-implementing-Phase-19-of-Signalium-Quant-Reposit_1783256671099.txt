======================================================================
MISSION
======================================================================

Build the Institutional Research Intelligence and Continuous Learning
Governance Platform.

This platform governs the evolution of Signalium Quant.

It continuously monitors market behaviour, datasets, features, models,
labels, and production performance.

It does NOT automatically retrain models.

Instead, it performs institutional-grade research monitoring and
recommends actions.

Maintain complete backward compatibility.
