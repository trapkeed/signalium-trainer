signalium-trainer

• BUY
• SELL
• NO TRADE

We are extending the model into a multi-task quantitative prediction model.

The shared feature encoder and backbone must remain intact.

Do NOT redesign the entire network.

Instead, add specialized prediction heads that share the same learned market representation.

======================================================================
MISSION
======================================================================

Extend the Signalium Quant Model so that a single forward pass predicts:

• Market direction
• Expected move size
• Suggested stop loss
• Suggested take profit
• Expected holding time
• Confidence score
• Probability that Take Profit is reached before Stop Loss

The model must learn these tasks jointly.
