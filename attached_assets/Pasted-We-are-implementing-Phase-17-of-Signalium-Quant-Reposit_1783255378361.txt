======================================================================
MISSION
======================================================================

Build the Adaptive Ensemble Intelligence Platform (AEIP).

Signalium must evolve from a single-model forecasting system into an adaptive ensemble of specialized quantitative models.

Every model should learn different market behaviours.

The platform should intelligently combine model outputs to produce a final institutional-grade forecast.

Maintain complete backward compatibility.

Do NOT remove any existing model.
