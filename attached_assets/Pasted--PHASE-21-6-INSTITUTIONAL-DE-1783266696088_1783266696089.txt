======================================================================
MISSION
======================================================================

Build the Institutional Decision Memory and Self-Evaluation Engine.

Signalium must permanently remember every decision it has ever made.

The objective is to enable institutional-grade decision analytics,
decision auditing, continuous self-evaluation, and long-term decision
quality improvement.

This system is NOT intended to retrain models automatically.

Instead, it becomes Signalium's permanent institutional memory of every
forecast, recommendation, execution plan, lifecycle transition and
outcome.

Maintain complete backward compatibility.
