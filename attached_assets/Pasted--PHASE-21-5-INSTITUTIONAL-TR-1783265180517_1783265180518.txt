======================================================================
PHASE 21.5
INSTITUTIONAL TRADE LIFECYCLE INTELLIGENCE ENGINE (TLIE)
======================================================================

Repository:

signalium-trainer

======================================================================
MISSION
======================================================================

Extend the Execution Intelligence Platform by implementing a complete
Trade Lifecycle Intelligence Engine.

Signalium must no longer treat a trade as a simple BUY or SELL.

Instead, every trade becomes a living object whose state evolves from
idea generation to final closure.

The engine must continuously evaluate market conditions and recommend
the appropriate lifecycle transition.

Maintain complete backward compatibility.

Do NOT execute trades.

Produce recommendations only.

======================================================================

======================================================================

Implement the following lifecycle states.

↓

↓

↓

All required conditions satisfied.

↓

PENDING ENTRY

Waiting for optimal execution.

↓

ACTIVE

Position opened.

↓

POSITION BUILDING

Optional scaling into position.

↓

PROTECTED

Stop moved to breakeven.

↓

PARTIAL EXIT

Partial profit secured.

↓

RUNNER

Remaining position allowed to run.

↓

TRAILING

Dynamic trailing stop active.

↓

EXIT READY

Exit conditions developing.

↓

CLOSING

Trade being finalized.

↓

CLOSED

Trade completed.
