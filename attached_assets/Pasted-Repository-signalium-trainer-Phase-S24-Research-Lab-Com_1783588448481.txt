Phase S24 — Research Lab Completion

Implement only missing functionality.
Detect existing implementations first and extend them rather than duplicating logic.

This phase finalizes the Research Lab and transforms it into the administrative and scientific control center for the Signalium Quant Decision Engine.

The Research Lab is never public facing.
It has no retail users.
It has no frontend outside the internal admin research interface.

The Research Lab exists exclusively to improve the QDE model over time.

10. One Model Philosophy Enforcement
====================================

Validate architecture consistency.

* one QDE model
* one active version
* one live decision maker

* multiple experts
* voting systems
* ensemble routing
* expert weighting
* expert arbitration
* canary deployment routing

Shadow evaluation is allowed.
