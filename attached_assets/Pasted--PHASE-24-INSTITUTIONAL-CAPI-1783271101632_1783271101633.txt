======================================================================
MISSION
======================================================================

Build Signalium's Institutional Capital Allocation & Risk Intelligence
Platform.

The objective is to transform Signalium from a forecasting engine into
an institutional portfolio management system capable of allocating
capital dynamically according to model quality, market conditions,
portfolio exposure, risk budgets and expected performance.

This system consumes forecasts, execution recommendations and portfolio
analytics but never changes the underlying forecasting models.

Maintain complete backward compatibility.
